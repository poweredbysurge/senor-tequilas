The client delivered their photo library. The files listed below are now in this project's assets. Replace the named `PHOTO:` placeholders with the file given, page by page. Do this in the chosen concept, desktop and mobile, and do not touch anything that is not a photo slot.

### Rules for this pass

1. **Only the slots listed here change.** Every other `PHOTO:` placeholder stays exactly as it is. We do not have those shots yet and a wrong photo is worse than a labeled box.
2. **Crop, never stretch.** Fit each photo to its slot by cropping. Keep the subject in frame. Portrait phone photos going into wide slots get a center crop; tell me if a crop loses the subject and we will pick a different file.
3. **Text on photos still needs a panel or a gradient.** Several of these are bright and busy. Contrast is not optional.
4. **Alt text is the real dish or room name**, taken from the copy on that page, not the file name.
5. **No faces as heroes on the events pages.** Guests in the watch-party and celebration photos are fine in a grid or a strip, not blown up as a hero, until the client confirms he has consent. The two files marked "do not use" in the client's own naming are not in this set for that reason.
6. **Where a slot says "confirm room," place the photo but add a small `CONFIRM ROOM` tag** in the corner, the same way the placeholders are labeled. Roberto will tell us which room it is.
