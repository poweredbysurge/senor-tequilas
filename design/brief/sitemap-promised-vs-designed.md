## What I'd flag from the board

1. **You have one concept on the interior pages, not two.** The master board's own intro says the Mercado Folklore theme was dropped from the interiors and Batches 1 to 5 run the dark system only. That's actually consistent with the kickoff, where the dark theme was locked with the client, so I wouldn't reverse it. But it's not what you asked me to write the prompt for, so decide on purpose rather than by accident.
2. **Three homepage concepts exist** (Modern Dark v2, Retro Poster, Mercado Market) but Sam told the client on the call that the next round would be "the site we think you should move forward with, plus an alternate hero." Three homepages is more than promised; that's fine, just present it as a choice, not a delay.
3. **Mobile is not done anywhere.** The board notes it. 95% of their traffic is phones and Roberto made a point of it on the call. Once the direction is picked, mobile is the next design pass and it's the one the client will judge.
