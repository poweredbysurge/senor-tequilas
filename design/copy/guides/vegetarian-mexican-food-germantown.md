# Vegetarian and Meatless Mexican Food in Germantown, MD: What's on the Menu

Four mains, five starters, every dessert and the seafood for Lent Fridays, with straight notes on what is not vegan.

Señor Tequila's has a vegetarian section on [the menu](/menu) with four mains, five vegetarian appetizers scattered through the rest of it, every dessert meat-free, and four seafood plates for anyone keeping meatless Fridays. Lent 2027 runs from Ash Wednesday, February 10, to Easter Sunday, March 28, so this list is as much a Friday plan as a vegetarian one.

What we do not have is a vegan menu. A few dishes come close, and the questions worth asking your server are below.

## The vegetarian mains

Four plates, all marked V on the menu:

- **Enchiladas de Espinaca.** Three spinach and cheese enchiladas drenched in flor de calabaza (squash blossom) cream sauce, topped with queso fresco and sour cream.
- **Garden Fajita.** A sizzling skillet of zucchini, yellow squash, carrots, onions, mushrooms, bell peppers and tomatoes. More on the [fajitas and molcajetes page](/fajitas-molcajetes).
- **Garden Burrito.** The same vegetables rolled in a flour tortilla, topped with salsa verde, lettuce, pico de gallo and sour cream.
- **Dos Chiles Rellenos.** Two roasted poblanos stuffed with cheese, egg-battered and fried, over rice with salsa roja and sour cream. Un Chile Relleno, the single, is on the appetizer list.

## Vegetarian starters and sides

- **Guacamole Fresco Bowl**, 8 ounces, made with avocado, cilantro, tomato, onion, lime and salt.
- **Queso Bowl**, 8 ounces of the house white cheese dip (skip the chorizo add-on).
- **Esquites**, grilled street corn with mayo, Tajín, cotija, cilantro and lime.
- **Plátanos Fritos**, nine fried sweet plantain slices with sour cream and cotija, with black or refried beans.
- **Un Chile Relleno**, one stuffed poblano over rice.

At [happy hour](/happy-hour), Monday to Friday 3 to 6, the vegetarian picks are Chips and Queso, Platanitos and Esquites, each $6.

## Meatless Fridays: the seafood

For Lent, or any Friday, the seafood list:

- **Frida's Salmon.** An 8-ounce grilled fillet over zucchini, squash and carrots with flor de calabaza sauce.
- **Tacos de la Costa.** Crispy breaded shrimp tacos with pico de gallo, coleslaw, chipotle ranch and cotija.
- **Enchiladas de Camarón.** Three shrimp enchiladas in flor de calabaza cream sauce.
- **Seasonal Ceviche.** Changes with what is in. Ask your server what it is this week.

There are no fish tacos on the menu, only shrimp. If you are searching "fish tacos near me" for Friday, the honest answer is Tacos de la Costa or the salmon.

## Dessert is all vegetarian

Everything in [La Dulcería](/la-dulceria) is marked V: churros with chocolate and caramel, flan, tres leches, the Cheesecake Chimichanga, the Volcán de Xocolate (warm chocolate cake, melted center, vanilla ice cream), fried ice cream and the Oreo or strawberry Malteadas. The aguas frescas, horchata and jamaica, are house-made, and the Virgin Mangonada is mango, chamoy and Tajín with a mango paleta.

## Kids who don't eat meat

Pa' los Niños, the kids' menu, has mac and cheese and a cheese quesadilla (order it without the chicken), each with rice and beans or fries.

## Before you order: what to ask

There is no vegan menu, and some answers depend on how a dish is prepared, so ask your server before you order. The useful questions are how the beans and rice are cooked, what goes into the flour tortillas, and whether the fryer is shared with meat. The kitchen will tell you straight. If you skip dairy, ask for the Garden Burrito without the sour cream.

## How we cook

Señor Tequila's is owned by brothers Roberto and Alex Rivas, family-owned since 2002 and on Century Boulevard in Germantown since 2015. Tortillas are pressed in-house and the desserts are made here. Google has the restaurant at 4.6 stars from more than 5,700 reviews.

## Vegetarian questions

**Does Señor Tequila's have vegan options?**

There is no vegan menu. The Guacamole Fresco Bowl is avocado, cilantro, tomato, onion, lime and salt, and the Garden Fajita is a skillet of vegetables. For beans, rice and tortillas, ask your server how they are prepared.

**What can I eat for Lent on a Friday?**

Four seafood plates, Frida's Salmon, Tacos de la Costa, Enchiladas de Camarón and the Seasonal Ceviche, plus the four vegetarian mains and every dessert. Lent 2027 is February 10 to March 28.

**Do you have fish tacos?**

No. The shrimp tacos, Tacos de la Costa, are the closest. Frida's Salmon is the fish plate.

**Are there gluten-free vegetarian dishes?**

The menu marks gluten-free items with a GF label. Tell your server you want vegetarian and gluten-free and they will point out the dishes that are both.

**Is there a vegetarian kids' meal?**

Yes. Mac and cheese, or a cheese quesadilla without chicken, with rice and beans or fries.
