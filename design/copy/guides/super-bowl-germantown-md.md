# Where to Watch the Super Bowl in Germantown, MD: Sunday, February 14, 2027

Three ways to watch, five things to check before you pick a bar, and where the sound is all the way up.

<!-- TODO(kickoff): update when the NFL announces the time -->

Super Bowl LXI is on Sunday, February 14, 2027, from SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, on ABC and ESPN in English and ESPN Deportes and Univision in Spanish. The NFL had not published kickoff as of November 2026. Recent Super Bowls kicked off around 6:30 PM Eastern, so plan on a 6:30 start and a finish after 10. It is also Valentine's Day, which changes the math for a lot of couples.

Short version: in Germantown you have three choices, your couch, a chain sports bar, or the bar area at Señor Tequila's, where every seat faces a screen, the sound is up, and the bar stays open until the game is over. Here is how to decide, and what to check wherever you go.

## Three ways to watch in Germantown

**At home.** Cheapest, and you control the remote. The catch is the food. If you are feeding more than eight, a catered taco bar costs from $22 a person, delivered, and nobody has to cook. Order 48 hours ahead.

**A chain sports bar.** Screens are not the problem there. Ask two things before you go: whether there is a wait for a table by 5, and whether the kitchen serves through the fourth quarter or drops to a snack list.

**A restaurant with a real bar area.** The middle option: table service, a kitchen that serves through the game, and the sound on. That is what [the bar area at Señor Tequila's](/bar) is built for.

## What to check at any bar before Super Bowl Sunday

1. **Is the sound on?** A bar with twelve screens and the jukebox on is not a watch party. Here the Super Bowl plays in full surround sound.
2. **Can you reserve?** Most bars go first come, first served for the Super Bowl, and so do we. Big games fill the bar area before kickoff, so come early.
3. **How late is the kitchen open?** The game will run past 10 PM. Ask whether the menu runs to the final whistle or drops to a snack list at 9. Here the bar stays open until the Super Bowl is over, with a late night menu available.
4. **Can kids come?** Our bar area is all ages on Sundays. The 21-and-over rule is Friday and Saturday after 9 PM only.
5. **Where do you park?** Free lot at the door, next to Topgolf off I-270.

## The room at Señor Tequila's

The bar area seats 108: sixteen four-top tables, two 12-seat long tables and a 20-seat counter, with 13-plus TVs. The two long tables are the ones to aim for with a crew of eight to twelve. More than twelve? Call ahead at (301) 569-4574 so we know you are coming. Private rooms are not booked on Valentine's Day, so the long tables are the group option that night.

Screens also run in the International Room and the patio, most likely with the sound off, which is where the half-watching half of the table ends up. The Mexican Room has no TVs, and live mariachi plays there from 7 to 9 PM that night, so a Valentine's dinner and the Super Bowl can happen under one roof. There is a [page for Valentine's Day dinner](/valentines-day) on its own.
