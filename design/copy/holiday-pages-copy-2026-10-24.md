### SECTION: The birria
**Eyebrow:** Eight hours before it meets a noodle
**H2:** How the Birria Is Made
Tender beef, braised for eight hours with chile guajillo, chile de árbol, cloves, garlic, and the rest of a spice list the brothers are not going to type out for a website. The meat falls apart. The liquid it cooked in becomes the consommé, and that consommé is why the bowl works: it is the same red broth you dip a quesabirria taco into, except here the noodles soak it up for you.
If you know our [birria tacos and quesabirria](/birria-tacos/), this is the same braise in a different shape. If you do not, start with the ramen. It is the one that makes people take a photo first.
The Birria Pizza is moving to takeout only, so if that was your order, the [takeout page](/takeout-delivery/) is where it lives now.

### SECTION: The story
**Eyebrow:** 2020
**H2:** Fifteen Recipes and the Trunk of a Car
Alex Rivas loves caldos. Stews, broths, anything that takes a day. During the 2020 lockdown, with the dining rooms dark, he started cooking birria at home and testing it on the one critic he trusted.
Every two days he drove to his brother Roberto's house with a fresh batch of birria and a stack of handmade tortillas, and left them on the trunk of Roberto's car, because that was the closest two people were allowed to get. Roberto ate. Alex adjusted. About fifteen recipes later, one of them was the one.
It went on the menu in 2021. It has been a top ten seller since. Frequent guests mention it way too often, which we take as a compliment.

### SECTION: FAQ
**H2:** Birria Ramen, Answered
**What is birria ramen?**
Birria ramen is a bowl of Japanese ramen noodles in birria consommé, topped with the braised birria meat, corn, radish, onion and cilantro, with lime on the side. At Señor Tequila's the birria is beef braised for eight hours with guajillo, chile de árbol, cloves and garlic. It has been on the menu since 2021.
**How much is the birria ramen, and how big is it?**
$21, and it is a full meal for one person. Most people do not finish the bowl. It is not on the happy hour menu. It is available at full price any time we are open.
**Is birria ramen spicy?**
Ours is mild as served. The guajillo and chile de árbol in the braise are there for depth, not heat. If you want it hotter, ask for salsa diabla and we will bring it to the table or pack it with your takeout.
**Does the birria ramen have allergens?**
The ramen noodles are wheat. Tell your server about any other allergy and the kitchen will walk you through the bowl.
**Can I get birria ramen for takeout or delivery?**
Yes. It travels well because the consommé is packed separately. Order pickup through Toast up to 15 minutes before close, or delivery through DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub.
**What is the difference between birria ramen and birria tacos?**
Same eight-hour beef braise, same consommé. The tacos put the meat in a handmade tortilla with the consommé on the side for dipping. The ramen puts the meat and consommé over noodles so the broth is in every bite. Many guests order one of each for the table.
**What should I drink with birria ramen?**
Something cold and carbonated: a michelada, a Mexican Coke, or a Negra Modelo. The bowl is rich, and the bubbles reset your palate between bites.
