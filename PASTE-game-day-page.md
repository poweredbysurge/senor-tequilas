## The strategy

**They are not a sports bar and should not try to be one.** Roberto said it himself on the 14th: in Germantown the "sports bar" competition is Applebee's and the Topgolf bar, and nobody dedicated. That is the opening. Competing for "sports bar near me" head-on is still a loss against the chains; competing for every specific question a fan in Germantown actually types is nearly uncontested.

**Soccer leads, but the framing changes.** The first draft called it "a Mexico bar." Roberto corrected that: Liga MX is on but the following is small, European matches draw more, and the World Cup and El Tri are what packed the room. So the soccer section is "every match, every league": World Cup and Mexico as the proof, Premier League and Champions League as the weekly draw, Liga MX named because the keyword is free. Then NFL as the second act, because "where to watch commanders game" is the biggest zero-difficulty term on the list and the season is on.

**Say "every NFL game," never "Sunday Ticket."** They have it. Roberto does not want to be branded as a Sunday Ticket venue, "every NFL game, every Sunday" carries the same promise, and Ahrefs agrees: nobody searches for Sunday Ticket bars. What they search for is their team. "Where to watch eagles game" is 12,000 a month, Steelers 8,500, Ravens 3,800, Commanders 1,900, all zero difficulty, and "steelers bar near me" and "eagles bar near me" are transplant fans looking for a home. So the NFL section gets a short block per team, and the page positions the bar area as where Eagles, Steelers and Ravens fans in Montgomery County watch, not only the local team.

**Fight nights are real.** They show the big fights, Canelo-level, with sound. That is its own short section now, not a placeholder.

**The game day special is the Sunday all-day happy hour.** Roberto proposed it, Jeremy agreed it launches Sept 28, bar area only. It answers "game day specials near me" and gives the NFL section a reason to exist beyond the TVs. It is not "happy hour whenever football is on"; Roberto ruled that out because it would drag Thursday and Monday nights in.

**One page, not two.** The nav is full. Soccer and NFL get their own H2 sections with jump links.

**The proof is already shot.** The photo library has 20 photographs and 31 videos of the World Cup watch parties. Existing posters cover only World Cup and football, so the page's visuals come from that set. Wide crowd shots rather than close-ups until Roberto confirms consent.

**The bigger local-search lever is not the page.** Google's local pack answers "sports bar near me." Adding **Sports bar** as a secondary Business Profile category plus posts of the watch party photos does more for that query than any page can. On the local SEO list.
